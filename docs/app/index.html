Anyone can prove a Sepolia event to Creditcoin — there is no privileged relayer. Paste a CollateralWithdrawn transaction hash from the vault, and this page will fetch its inclusion and continuity proof from the Attestcoin prover and submit it to DeadswitchManagerV3.execute() with your wallet. If the attested collateral is below the threshold, the position liquidates in the same transaction that verifies the proof.
